# Privacy Policy (Music Like)

Last updated: 2026-02-14

Music Like ("the App") respects user privacy and handles information in accordance with applicable laws and the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App primarily handles the following information:

- **Local audio file information** (file names, paths, metadata, etc.)
  - Purpose: library display, playback, search, lyrics synchronization
  - Source: only files/folders explicitly selected by the user
- **Advertising-related information** (advertising identifiers, etc.)
  - Purpose: ad delivery, fraud prevention, ad optimization
  - Shared with: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
- **Notification permission status**
  - Purpose: control of playback notifications

The App does not, by default, upload contacts, location, or camera data to our servers.

## 2. Purpose of Use

Collected information is used to:

- Provide music playback and library management features
- Display lyrics and synchronize them with playback
- Serve ads, measure ad performance, and maintain the service
- Troubleshoot and improve quality

## 3. Third-Party Sharing

The App uses Google services (AdMob) for ad delivery. Ad partners may handle device identifiers and other data. The App may present Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form and will request ads only according to the user's consent choice.

- Google Ads Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### UMP display timing

- **First launch**: When the app is launched for the first time, the UMP consent form is shown to obtain consent for ad delivery. Personalized ad requests will not be made until consent is granted.
- **Settings changes**: If the user changes ad/privacy settings within the app, the consent flow may be re-displayed to obtain an updated consent state.
- **Consent re-check**: If consent expires or policy changes require re-consent, the UMP may be shown again.

Users may refuse or withdraw consent. If consent is refused, the App will either serve non-personalized ads or suppress ad requests according to the user's choice. Consent state is stored on-device and controls ad initialization and requests.

## 4. Permissions and User Controls

The App requests the following permissions when necessary:

- Access to audio/media files (for library import)
- Notification permission (for playback notifications)

Users may change permission grants in device settings. If a permission is denied, related features may be unavailable.

## 5. Data Retention

Settings and library information stored in the App are retained until the user uninstalls the App or clears the app data.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The app's target age is set in the Google Play Console. If the app targets children under the applicable age threshold, we will comply with relevant laws (e.g., COPPA) and Google Play family policies.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable measures to prevent data leakage, loss, and damage. However, internet transmission and device environments carry inherent risks that cannot be fully eliminated.

## 8. Changes

This policy may be updated as needed. Significant changes will be announced in-app or on the distribution page.
